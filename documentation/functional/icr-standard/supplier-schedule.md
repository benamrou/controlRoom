# Supplier schedule

## Purpose

Maintain **supplier delivery calendars** — holidays, recurring schedules, and generated schedule files feeding GOLD ordering/receiving.

## Who may use it

**Tech Services** (`USERSTECH`) primarily.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Holiday schedule | `/holiday` | Supplier holiday non-delivery days |
| Mgt by schedule | `/schedule` | Manage schedule definitions |
| Generate schedule | `/generateschedule` | Generate schedule output for a scope |
