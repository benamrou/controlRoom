What is this setup?

This screen registers a GOLD ERP environment as a retailer in the Supply Chain AI engine. Once saved, the AI engine can connect to the GOLD database, learn its business rules through Context Learning, and generate per-retailer views used for replenishment intelligence.

Before you begin: CORPENV row exists with ENVDBLINK and ENVGOLDSCHEMA set DB link is active and reachable from the ICR app DB Your user has USERAIADMIN = 1

What happens after saving?
1
Context Learning (S03) — teach the AI engine how your GOLD instance defines active items, inactive items, stock quantity, promotions, and POS sales. All 5 P1 items must be locked before views can be generated.
2
View generation — once P1 items are locked, the AI generates V_GOLD_ACTIVE_ITEM_{{ retailerId }} using your learned SQL conditions.
3
AI operations — the AI Dashboard, Blockers, and Recommendations screens become active for this retailer.
